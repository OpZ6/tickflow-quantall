# VCP iteration 3 development gates
The previously opened 2025-09-05 through 2026-06-30 period is diagnostic only and must not select iteration 3 rules. Mechanism selection uses 2021-09-06 through 2025-09-04 with year-by-year reporting and an all-stock point-in-time equal-weight benchmark. A frozen candidate requires a separate, previously unused historical or future test period.

Before external validation, a candidate must achieve annualized return at least 10%, Sharpe at least 0.8, maximum drawdown no worse than -20%, trade win rate at least 25%, return payoff ratio at least 2.0, monetary profit factor at least 1.30, average exposure at least 20%, and positive excess over the all-stock equal-weight benchmark. At least three full/partial calendar segments must be positive and no single winner may account for all net profit.

Iteration order is structural: broaden the market regime, then test two-close breakout confirmation, then require improving breadth. Position concentration may be changed only after entry quality passes; it cannot be used to manufacture annualized return from a weak signal.
